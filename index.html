About Us

Laitkor Nongdaneng is a serene and beautiful village located in the East Khasi Hills of Meghalaya. Known for its scenic landscapes and rich cultural heritage, the community here is warm and welcoming. We take pride in preserving our traditions while embracing new opportunities for growth and connection.
